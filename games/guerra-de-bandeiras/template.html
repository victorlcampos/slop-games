How it plays

Their flag scores when you carry it to your own stand — and only while your own flag is in it.
WASD or arrows to move · the mouse aims · click to fire · space rolls · M mutes
The camera sits on you and the field is bigger than the screen. By day you see the whole room around you — and nothing through a wall.
The gun aims itself: you point roughly and it finds the man.
On a phone the left half walks; the right half is the trigger. The 🌀 button rolls.
Every body you put down pays in shards; on your own ground they buy a gun.
With your flag gone the point does not count — and it does not come back on its own.
