Kings & Gears

Build the castle. Hide the king. Then find theirs.

The Kingdom still builds in stone and timber. The Machines cast their own armour.

You get coins and a plot seven by nine.

After that it is one shot each. The siege engine sits on top of the castle and drives.

Whoever buries the other crown wins.

Hold ◀ ▶ to drive the siege engine · drag or hover to aim · release to open the power gauge · tap again to fire
Keyboard: left and right drive · up and down aim · space opens the gauge and fires · 1-4 switch munitions · M mutes

Which crown?

You pick the arsenal. The other side gets what is left.
